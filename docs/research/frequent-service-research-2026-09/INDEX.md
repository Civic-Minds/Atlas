# Frequent-service research

**Status:** Research complete; product definition remains separate from this evidence set.

## Research question

How do agencies define frequent service, and what threshold and service span
should Atlas use for a comparable daytime measure?

## Sample

The source review covers 500 agency records, including 172 with documented
numeric definitions, 73 with qualitative or named definitions, and 255 where no
formal definition was found.

## Method

Review current agency system maps, rider guides, and published frequent-network
materials. Preserve each published tier in the catalog, then compare the
results with consistent Atlas route-pattern metrics.

## Results and interpretation

There is no universal definition. Published thresholds, service spans, days,
geography, and modes vary. Agency definitions often describe corridors or
customer-facing products rather than every route in both directions.

## Recommendation

The criteria brief recommends a weekday 7am–7pm measure with a maximum 15-minute
headway and no gap at either edge. This remains a proposed product rule, not a
new production commitment.

## Limitations and next steps

The Atlas route-level comparison is not equivalent to every agency's corridor
or network definition. STM and TransLink need stop- or corridor-level follow-up
before the comparison is treated as a direct match.
