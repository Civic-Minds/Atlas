# International expansion research

**Status:** Exploratory; no country launch is authorized by this research.

## Research question

Which countries and feed-publishing models can Atlas support next without
introducing an unvalidated pipeline assumption or publishing unreviewed data?

## Sample

The brief covers candidate feeds and publishing models in Mexico, the United
Kingdom, France, Belgium, Spain, and Australia, with additional country leads.

## Method

Review official or authoritative feed portals, inspect feed structure and
licensing, run local dry-run validation where applicable, and record country-
specific blockers separately from general Atlas pipeline issues.

## Results and interpretation

Per-operator feeds in France, Belgium, and parts of Spain fit Atlas's current
ingestion model more closely than bundled regional feeds in the UK and parts of
Australia. Feed quality, licensing, and country-wide coverage still vary.

## Recommendation

Keep new-country candidates hidden until a country-level rollout is reviewed;
do not treat one technically valid agency as a complete country launch.

## Limitations and next steps

Candidate feed availability and licensing change over time. Recheck the source
before processing or publishing any candidate.
