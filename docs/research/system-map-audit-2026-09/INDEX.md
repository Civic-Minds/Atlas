# System-map audit

**Status:** Research complete for the reviewed sample.

## Research question

What do current official system maps call frequent service, and what thresholds
or service spans do those maps actually publish?

## Sample

142 agencies reviewed against current official system maps or approved rider
guides on September 18, 2026.

## Method

Use a current official system map, map legend, or approved rider-guide map as
the evidence standard. Retain every named map-defined tier and use a general or
representative tier only for aggregate charts.

## Results and interpretation

The report distinguishes numeric definitions, qualitative definitions, maps
without a frequent-service definition, and unavailable maps. Express, peak-only,
rail-only, and secondary tiers remain separate context rather than being
collapsed into one threshold.

## Limitations and next steps

The audit describes map language, not GTFS performance. It should be read with
the frequent-service catalog and Atlas comparison as separate evidence layers.
