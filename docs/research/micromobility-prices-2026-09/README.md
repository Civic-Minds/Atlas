# Micromobility prices

**Status:** prices are as-of their check dates and not independently verified by Atlas. 12 records are marked `verified` by the source repo (operator pages); 47 are `app_observation` (a price seen in the Lime app, not an official page).

Snapshot date: `2026-09-17`
Source: a separate local git repository (17 commits, no remote), last commit `303a54c` (2026-09-17). The commit list is in [`source-git-log.txt`](source-git-log.txt).
Scope: 59 pricing records for bike-share and scooter-share plans in 41 cities (Toronto, Montreal, New York, London and Paris as official seeds; Lime app observations across North America and Europe).

## How this relates to Atlas

Atlas does not use this data. It is a standalone side study on what bike-share and scooter-share cost, kept here so it is not lost. No Atlas product decision depends on it, and Atlas code and data were not changed by it. Micromobility is not part of the transit feeds Atlas publishes.

## Dates on the figures

Every record carries `checkedOn` (the date the source was read) and some carry `effectiveFrom` (the date the plan took effect). Newest and oldest `checkedOn` are both `2026-09-17`, so every price is a single-day snapshot. Only 3 records have an `effectiveFrom` (2026-01-05 to 2026-01-28). Prices are in local currency and Lime figures are often before tax. Treat any price as stale after a few months.
