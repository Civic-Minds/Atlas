# North America on-demand service audit

Status: in progress. Scope is Atlas agencies in North America. A match must be a current, regularly available service that the general public can use. ADA/paratransit-only services, proposals, stale pages, and explicitly active pilots are tracked separately.

### California excluded or not confirmed

- AC Transit Flex: suspended since the pandemic — [agency page](https://www.actransit.org/planning/ac-transit-flex).
- SacRT Flex: targeted access service, not open universal service — [Access for All](https://www.sacrt.com/planning-construction/accessforall/).
- OCTA OC Flex: old launch material describes a pilot; no current rider service was found.
- Marin Transit Connect: current evidence found only older service material and newer Marin Access programs; not confirmed current as a universal service.
- County Connection, WestCAT, SolTrans, VTA, Fresno Area Express, and Sonoma County Transit/Santa Rosa CityBus: current evidence found was planning, eligibility-limited, or insufficient to confirm a regular universal on-demand service.
