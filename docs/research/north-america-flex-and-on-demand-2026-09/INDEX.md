# North America Flex and on-demand audit

**Status:** Research complete; no data has been published from this audit.

## Research question

Which Atlas agencies operate current public on-demand services, and which of
those services publish GTFS-Flex or equivalent open data?

## Sample

The service audit covers 104 confirmed public agency/service records across
North America. The Flex audit maps those records into 100 ledger rows because
five exo sectors are grouped into one record.

## Method

Verify current agency or public-agency service pages, exclude ADA/paratransit-
only services and proposals, then check agency and municipal open-data portals
for GTFS-Flex or related geometry and booking-rule data.

## Results and interpretation

Three services have a confirmed current public GTFS-Flex publication. Many
other services publish useful zones, stops, or booking information without a
GTFS-Flex feed, so feed availability is a separate gap from service existence.

## Recommendation

Treat service confirmation and data-publication status as separate records.
Request missing zones, virtual stops, and booking rules directly when a public
feed is unavailable.

## Limitations and next steps

Publication status is time-sensitive and should be rechecked before any Atlas
overlay or importer work begins.
